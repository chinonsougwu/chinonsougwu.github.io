Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

This is my personal portfolio. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. I don't collect any information about you.

What your browser stores

To remember your choices, the site saves a few small settings in your own browser. They never leave your device and I can't see them.

  • Sound on or off, so the site remembers your choice next time.
  • Achievements you've unlocked, so each one only pops up once.
  • Which cartridges are out in the claw machine. This is cleared when you close the tab.

You can delete these at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, whether or not they have a GitHub account. I don't have access to those logs. You can read how GitHub handles this data in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Other websites

Fonts, sounds and images all load from this site itself, so browsing it doesn't send requests to other companies.

Some links lead elsewhere, such as GitHub, WhatsApp or your email app. Once you follow one, that service's own privacy policy applies.

If you contact me

If you email or message me through the links in the contact section, I'll only use your details to reply to you. I won't share them or add you to any mailing list.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
